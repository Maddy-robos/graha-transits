Board glyphs

Transit
A graha changes sign (gochara / ingress).
Retrograde
A graha turns backward (retrograde station).
Direct
A graha resumes forward motion (direct station).
Combustion
A graha too close to the Sun — “burnt”.

Vedic terms

Gochara
The ongoing transit of grahas through the rasis.
Drishti
A graha’s aspect — the rasis it “sees”. Counted inclusively: the 1st rasi is its own.
Rasi
Sign (Mesha … Meena). Switch Sanskrit / English with the अ / A button.
Guru / Shani
Jupiter / Saturn — the two slow grahas that define the double transit.

Double transit

What it is
Rasis influenced by both Guru and Shani at the same time.
“sits”
The graha is physically in that rasi.
“aspects”
The graha reaches it by drishti (aspect), without being there.
Why it changes
Recomputed whenever Guru or Shani changes sign, or Shani turns retrograde / direct.
